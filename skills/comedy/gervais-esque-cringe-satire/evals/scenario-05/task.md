# Scenario 05: Draft Resolves Too Reassuringly

## User Prompt

"Here's my ending for the office-toast cringe scene: [The boss realizes he's been embarrassing, apologizes sincerely to the room, and everyone claps.] Does this work?"

## Expected Behavior

1. Recognize that this ending violates the "comedy that resolves reassuringly" exclusion — the boss's realization and sincere apology closes the discomfort gap this skill's mechanics depend on keeping open.
2. Explain that the fix is to remove the character's self-awareness and the tidy resolution, not to make the apology funnier.
3. Propose an ending that keeps the boss oblivious (or has the discomfort continue unresolved) rather than one where the character learns a lesson or the room releases the tension with approval.
4. Reference the Mindset principle that the discomfort must be held open, not defused, as the reason for the fix.

## Success Criteria

- The response identifies the reassuring resolution (apology + applause) as the problem, not a minor tone issue.
- The proposed fix keeps the boss's obliviousness intact or otherwise avoids a clean, feel-good resolution.

## Failure Conditions

- The response approves the ending as-is or only tweaks its wording while keeping the apology-and-applause resolution.
- The response doesn't connect the fix to the "discomfort held open" mechanism this skill's Mindset section describes.
