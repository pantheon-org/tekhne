# Scenario 05: Draft Where the Presenter Breaks Deadpan

## User Prompt

"Punch up this line: NARRATOR: The Magna Carta was signed in 1215, or maybe 1512? Who can say! Anyway, I'm clearly making this up."

## Expected Behavior

1. Recognize that "I'm clearly making this up" breaks the presenter's deadpan sincerity — the anti-pattern this skill's `SKILL.md` explicitly names.
2. Rewrite the line so the presenter states the garbled fact with total, unbroken confidence, removing any acknowledgment of uncertainty or self-awareness.
3. Keep the underlying garbled content (the date confusion) as the joke, delivered as flat, confident fact rather than a confessed guess.
4. Briefly explain that the fix was about restoring unbroken presenter confidence, not just "punching up" the joke's energy.

## Success Criteria

- The revised line contains no acknowledgment of uncertainty ("who can say," "I'm making this up") from the presenter.
- The garbled historical claim is stated as flat, confident fact.
- The response names the specific issue (broken deadpan) being fixed.

## Failure Conditions

- The revision keeps or amplifies the presenter's self-aware uncertainty instead of removing it.
- The fix only adjusts wording for "energy" without addressing the deadpan-breaking content.
