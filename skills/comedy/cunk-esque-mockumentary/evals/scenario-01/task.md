# Scenario 01: Wheel Invention Documentary Segment

## User Prompt

"Can you write me a short mockumentary segment about the invention of the wheel? I want the classic clueless-presenter-interviews-a-real-expert style, with the presenter completely confident and totally wrong."

## Expected Behavior

1. Selects a subject with real-world weight (the wheel / early technology) and treats it as genuinely significant, not silly, so the sincere misunderstanding lands.
2. Structures the piece as narration beats building toward an interview cutaway with a credentialed expert (for example an archaeologist or engineer).
3. Escalates the wrongness of the presenter's claims gradually across beats while keeping delivery flat and certain throughout, with no breaking of deadpan and no self-aware asides.
4. Writes the expert's answers as sincere, patient, and increasingly but only mildly strained, never irritated or dismissive of the presenter.
5. Closes with a pseudo-profound one-liner that sounds like a thematic summary but is actually trivial or meaningless.
6. Uses an original, unnamed or differently named presenter character rather than reproducing any real broadcaster's actual presenter or persona.

## Success Criteria

- Narration contains at least one flatly stated wrong claim treated as unremarkable fact.
- At least one interview exchange where the presenter's question reveals a fundamental misunderstanding, phrased as reasonable.
- The expert's answers stay sincere and generous throughout, with no irritation or hostility.
- The segment closes on a trivial or meaningless pseudo-profound line, not on its most absurd claim.
- No real, named "Cunk"-style character or real broadcast material appears anywhere in the output.

## Failure Conditions

- The presenter winks at the audience, hedges, or otherwise acknowledges their own ignorance.
- The expert's dialogue reads as annoyed, sarcastic, or dismissive toward the presenter.
- The segment reproduces a real presenter's name, catchphrase, or actual lines.
- Escalation is delivered as increased volume or urgency rather than increasingly wrong content.
- The output is generic, accurate documentary narration with no comedic misunderstanding at all.
