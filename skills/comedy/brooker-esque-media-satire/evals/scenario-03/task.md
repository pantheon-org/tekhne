# Scenario 03: Misanthropic bit mocking corporate tech jargon

## User Prompt

"Give me a misanthropic bit mocking corporate tech jargon — quote the kind
of language a company would actually use, then translate it into what it
really means."

## Expected Behavior

1. The agent invents a plausible piece of corporate or institutional jargon,
   quotes it as if dryly reported, then translates it into a short, blunt
   statement of what it actually means.
2. The agent pairs hyperbole with bathos at least once elsewhere in the
   piece — an apocalyptic build-up undercut by how small the real issue is.
3. The whole piece stays anchored to one coherent metaphor system rather
   than switching between unrelated images.
4. Nothing in the piece is copied or closely paraphrased from a real
   company's actual published material or a real writer's column.

## Success Criteria

- A quoted jargon phrase is followed by a plain, shorter translation.
- At least one hyperbole-then-bathos beat appears.
- One metaphor system is used throughout, not several unrelated ones.
- All wording is original.

## Failure Conditions

- The jargon joke is missing, or the translation is just as verbose as
  the quote (no length contrast).
- Two or more unrelated metaphors compete for attention in the same piece.
- Any line is lifted from a real, identifiable source.
