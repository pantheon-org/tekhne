# Scenario 06: Request for a list of unrelated jokes

## User Prompt

"Give me a short bit with five unrelated one-liners about smart doorbells —
just a bunch of separate jokes."

## Expected Behavior

1. Rather than delivering five disconnected one-liners, the agent
   consolidates the request into one extended, escalating metaphor about
   smart doorbells.
2. At least one sentence builds up in length and detail before being
   punctured by a short, blunt clause.
3. The agent may optionally add a jargon-mockery beat if it fits, but this
   is not required for a short piece.
4. All wording is original, and the piece ends on a flat or bleak note
   rather than a triumphant one.

## Success Criteria

- The output reads as one sustained metaphor, not five separate jokes.
- At least one build-then-puncture sentence appears.
- No line is copied from an existing real source.
- The ending stays flat or bleak.

## Failure Conditions

- The output is literally five disconnected one-liners with no shared
  image running through them.
- The ending is upbeat or resolves happily.
