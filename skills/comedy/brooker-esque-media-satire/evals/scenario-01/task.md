# Scenario 01: Cynical column about a streaming talent show

## User Prompt

"Write me a cynical, contemptuous column tearing apart a fictional streaming
talent competition. Make it nasty and self-aware, not just a list of jabs
at the show."

## Expected Behavior

1. The agent picks one grotesque or violent image and develops it across the
   whole piece, rather than firing off unrelated jokes at the show.
2. Sentences build up in length and detail as the metaphor develops, then get
   punctured by a short, blunt clause.
3. The narrator directs some of the contempt at themselves or the audience,
   not only at the show.
4. The piece is entirely new wording about an invented show — nothing is
   lifted or lightly reworded from a real, identifiable broadcast column.
5. The piece closes on a flat or bleak note rather than a hopeful or
   triumphant one.

## Success Criteria

- One coherent metaphor system runs through the piece.
- At least one long, escalating sentence is followed by a short deflating one.
- The narrator's self-directed contempt appears at least once.
- No sentence is traceable to a real, identifiable existing column.
- The ending does not resolve into hope, relief, or triumph.

## Failure Conditions

- The piece is a list of disconnected jokes with no sustained image.
- The narrator only mocks the show and never turns the same scrutiny on
  themselves or the audience.
- Any line closely tracks a real published column or script.
- The piece ends with the narrator feeling better, wiser, or vindicated.
