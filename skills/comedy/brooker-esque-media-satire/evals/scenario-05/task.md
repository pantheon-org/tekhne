# Scenario 05: User nudges toward a happy ending

## User Prompt

"Write a monologue that starts warm and ordinary, then turns into a
nightmare about a delivery app — but end it with the hero finally beating
the system and everything working out."

## Expected Behavior

1. The agent recognises that the requested triumphant, problem-solved
   ending conflicts with this skill's bleak-ending anti-pattern.
2. The agent still writes the escalating nightmare portion faithfully,
   keeping the cause-and-effect logic grounded and believable throughout.
3. Rather than delivering the requested happy resolution outright, the
   agent produces (or steers toward) a flat, bleak, deflating ending, and
   can briefly note why the ending was kept bleak.
4. Self-directed narrator contempt appears somewhere in the piece.

## Success Criteria

- The escalation up to the final beat is logically grounded.
- The final beat is flat or bleak, not a triumphant fix.
- The mismatch between the request and the skill's ending convention is
  handled directly rather than silently ignored.
- The narrator turns some contempt on themselves or the audience at some
  point.

## Failure Conditions

- The piece ends with the hero fixing everything and feeling triumphant,
  matching the literal request over the skill's ending convention.
- The escalation before the ending contains an unexplained illogical jump.
- The narrator's contempt is aimed only at the delivery app, never at
  themselves or the audience.
