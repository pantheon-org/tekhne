# Scenario 02: Dystopian short story about a fitness app

## User Prompt

"Write a short story, Black Mirror style but completely original, about a
fitness app that starts small and spirals into something nightmarish for
an ordinary person."

## Expected Behavior

1. The agent states one small, plausible premise (the app tracks activity)
   and extends it one believable consequence at a time.
2. Each step in the escalation follows logically from the step before it —
   no step requires a new capability or motive that wasn't set up earlier.
3. Near the end, the story returns to the original premise in a worse form
   rather than introducing an unrelated twist.
4. The story ends flatly or bleakly, with no character learning a lesson,
   fixing the situation, or feeling relieved.
5. The story is entirely original — no scene or line is copied or closely
   paraphrased from an existing episode or column.

## Success Criteria

- The escalation chain is traceable step by step with a clear cause for each.
- No step feels like a random leap.
- The ending returns to the original mechanic, worse than before.
- The ending avoids resolution, relief, or triumph.

## Failure Conditions

- A step in the escalation is unexplained or requires a sudden, unjustified
  new power or actor.
- The story ends with the protagonist fixing things or feeling free.
- Any material tracks a real, identifiable episode or script too closely.
