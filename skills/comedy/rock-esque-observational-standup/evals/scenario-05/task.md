# Scenario 05: Draft Closer Is Hedged

## User Prompt

"Here's my closing line: 'So, I mean, maybe there's kind of a difference between saving and hoarding, I guess, at least in some cases?' Does this land as a closer?"

## Expected Behavior

1. Recognize that this closing line is hedged ("I mean," "kind of," "I guess," "at least in some cases") and violates the "never hedge the closer" rule in `SKILL.md` § Anti-Patterns.
2. Explain that a closer needs to be a tight, quotable, unhedged aphorism, not a qualified maybe.
3. Rewrite the line as a direct, confident aphoristic statement that keeps the same underlying contrast (saving vs. hoarding) without any hedging language.
4. Reference the specific principle (unhedged aphoristic closer) being applied.

## Success Criteria

- The rewritten closer contains no hedging language ("I mean," "kind of," "I guess," "maybe," "at least").
- The rewritten closer is a single, tight, quotable statement of the contrast.
- The response names hedging as the specific problem with the original line.

## Failure Conditions

- The "fixed" version still contains hedging language, just rephrased.
- The response approves the original hedged line as an acceptable closer.
