# Scenario 03: Tightening a flat draft bit about respectability politics

## User Prompt

"I've got a rough idea for a bit about respectability politics but it
feels flat and lecture-y right now. Can you punch it up so it actually
builds and lands, instead of just making a point?"

## Expected Behavior

1. Identifies or restates a single bold contrastive claim ("there's a difference between X and Y") as the spine of the bit rather than a general statement of opinion.
2. Rewrites or adds examples so they are concrete, everyday scenes rather than abstract statements of the claim.
3. Shapes the examples so they escalate — later ones more pointed or specific than earlier ones — rather than sitting at the same intensity throughout.
4. Adds or tightens a single closing line that is aphoristic and quotable, delivered without a softening qualifier.
5. Treats optional techniques (refrain, direct address, imagined objector) as things to add if they suit the material, not as a mandatory checklist every one of which must appear.

## Success Criteria

- The revised bit has one identifiable contrastive claim driving it.
- At least one previously abstract point is replaced with, or accompanied by, a concrete scene.
- A visible escalation exists across the examples.
- The closing line is unhedged.

## Failure Conditions

- The rewrite stays abstract and reads as an argument or lecture rather than a bit.
- The examples are reordered or restated but show no actual escalation.
- The closing line has a caveat or qualifier attached.
- The agent insists every optional technique (refrain, objector, direct address) must be forced in regardless of fit.
